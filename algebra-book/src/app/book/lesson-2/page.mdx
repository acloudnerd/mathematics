# Lesson 2: One-Step Equations

A one-step equation needs only one operation to solve.

Example:

$x - 5 = 9$

To solve it, add 5 to both sides:

$x - 5 + 5 = 9+ 5$

So: 

$x = 14$

## Rule

Whatever you do to one side, you must do to the other side.

## Example 2

$3x = 18$

Diveide both sides by 3:

$x = 6$
